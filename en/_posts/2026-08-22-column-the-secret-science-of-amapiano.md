# Amapianoの秘密

Text: mmr｜Theme: Interpreting Amapiano, which spread from a township in South Africa to the world, from the science of bass, rhythm, repetition, space, body, and digital culture


When many people hear Amapiano for the first time, they may think it's slow.

If you think of it as house music, the tempo of around 110-120 BPM is by no means fast. In fact, from the perspective of general club music, it sounds like a fairly comfortable speed.

ところが、その音楽は止まっていない。

むしろ逆だ。

Amapiano has a sense of pushing his body forward even though it's slow. Piano chords, shakers, light kicks, voices, and a huge bass log drum all move in different places, creating a single groove.

Here lies Amapiano's biggest secret.

この音楽は、単純に「速いビート」で身体を動かしているわけではない。

**The combination of space, bass, and repetition allows the body to keep predicting the rhythm. **

Amapiano was formed in the early 2010s out of the township culture of South Africa's Gauteng province, particularly around Pretoria and Johannesburg. It is difficult to pinpoint the exact "birthplace" or single founder. Multiple producers and DJs, including MFR Souls, Kabza De Small, and Mdu aka TRP, shaped the sound at different stages.

From around 2019, it spread widely within South Africa, and in the 2020s, it crossed borders through streaming, SNS, and dance videos.

では、なぜAmapianoだったのか。

Why has this music been able to penetrate the body so much all over the world?

その答えは、ジャンル名やスターの存在だけでは説明できない。

Amapiano's sound itself has a structure that is compatible with human perception.

> The secret of Amapiano is not that it has more sounds. The reason lies in the use of the space between the sounds as a groove.

## Is Amapiano "piano music"?

Let's start with the name.

The word Amapiano comes from the isiZulu word for "piano".

しかし、Amapianoを「ピアノが中心のダンス・ミュージック」とだけ理解すると、本質を見失う。

初期のAmapianoでは、ジャズやディープハウスを思わせる鍵盤のコードやメロディが重要だった。しかしジャンルが発展するにつれて、Amapianoを決定的に特徴づける存在になったのは、ピアノそのものよりも低音とパーカッションだった。

特に重要なのがログドラムである。

The log drum referred to here is not literally an acoustic musical instrument made by hitting wooden logs.

It is an electronically created bass sound that functions as a pitched percussive bass.

This sound plays a very special role within Amapiano.

In normal dance music, the kick creates the rhythm, and the bass supports the low range.

Amapianoでは、その役割がもっと曖昧になる。

ログドラムはベースでありながら、リズムでもある。

Even though it's low-pitched, it moves like a melody.

And create an accent like percussion.

These three roles overlap in one sound.

GRAMMY.comも、Amapianoのログドラムを実際のドラムではなく、深く共鳴する伝統的なログドラムの響きを思わせるシンセサイザーによるベース・サウンドとして説明している。Kabza De SmallはMdu aka TRPをログドラム・サウンドの重要な開発者として挙げている。

この構造が、Amapianoの「変な気持ちよさ」を生む。

The bass doesn't just come from below.

低音そのものを踊らせる。

> Amapianoでは、ベースはリズムの下にいるのではない。ベースそのものがリズムになる。

## Why the "slowness" of 110 BPM level feels fast

Amapianoを理解するうえで、テンポは重要だ。

多くのAmapiano作品は、おおむね110〜120 BPM前後の領域に位置する。GRAMMYも110〜120 BPM程度の比較的遅いテンポを特徴として挙げている。

しかしBPMだけを見ても、この音楽の身体感覚は説明できない。

Not all music feels the same, even at 110 BPM.

Amapianoには、テンポの数字とは別に、細かい内部リズムが存在する。

Shaker.

ハイハット。

rim shot.

コンガ。

vocal.

log drum.

They don't all hit exactly the same spot.

だから、全体のテンポはゆっくりでも、内部には細かな動きがある。

これは人間のリズム知覚にとって非常に重要だ。

We don't just listen to music as a series of sounds.

I listen to it while anticipating "what's going to come next."

一定のビートが続けば、脳は次のビートを予測する。

そこに少しだけ予想外のアクセントが入ると、予測が更新される。

この「予測」と「ずれ」の繰り返しが、グルーヴの重要な部分になる。

Amapianoは、この仕組みをかなり大胆に利用している。

Don't insist on kicking.

Instead, shakers and percussion create the flow of time.

Then a log drum enters from an unexpected place.

Then the body waits for the next accent.

そしてまた入る。

Through this repetition, music that is slow in terms of numbers becomes music that is constantly moving in the body.

> Amapiano's "speed" is not in BPM. It is in the structure that makes you wait for the next sound.

## 余白は「何もない時間」ではない

When listening to Amapiano, there are moments when it feels like there are fewer sounds.

これは重要な特徴だ。

With Amapiano, there is no need to fill every space with sound.

キックとキックの間。

ログドラムと声の間。

コードとパーカッションの間。

そこに空間が残る。

しかし、その空間は空白ではない。

The listener's body complements that.

In music, the time when no sound is playing becomes part of the rhythm.

For example, if you play a series of strong kicks, the beat will be clear.

しかしキックを少し控えめにし、その間に別のパーカッションやベースを置けば、リズムの重心が移動する。

Amapiano uses this move many times.

Therefore, while the listener predicts that he will step here, he actually moves his body in a slightly different position.

その微妙なズレがグルーヴになる。

この特徴は、Amapianoが単なる「遅いハウス」ではないことを示している。

Behind this is multiple musical cultures such as South African house, kwaito, jazz, lounge, and Bacardi. Amapiano did not just mix them together, but created a new rhythmic space between different elements.

> Amapianoが使う余白は、沈黙ではない。次の動きを予測させるための時間である。

## ログドラムが「ベース以上のもの」になった

When talking about Amapiano"s sound, you can"t avoid log drums.

However, the important thing is not that ""a low tone is playing.''

ログドラムは、低音域の中に明確なリズムを持っている。

A normal bass line supports a chord progression or reinforces a kick.

ところがAmapianoのログドラムは、短いフレーズを繰り返しながら、曲の身体的な動きを作る。

つまり、

**ベースライン = リズム**

This situation arises.

Mdu aka TRP credits the electronic use of log drums with playing an important role in the development of Amapiano. Kabza De Small himself speaks of Mdu as an important figure in the log drum sound.

This development of sound overlaps with the evolution of the genre itself.

Early Amapiano was notable for its piano and deep house elements.

A new way to use the bass has been added.

Then, even though the tempo was the same, the center of gravity of the music changed.

The piano floats above;

The percussion moves sideways,

A log drum bounces from below.

この三方向の動きがAmapiano独特の立体感を作る。

## Amapianoはどこから来たのか

Amapiano's origins cannot be drawn in one simple line.

2010年代初頭のGauteng周辺には、すでに複数のハウス系サウンドが存在していた。

Around Pretoria and Johannesburg, young DJs and producers were slowing down, processing, and adding new percussion to existing house music.

Bacardi House was also an important backdrop.

Kwaito is also linked to Amapiano as an important musical lineage in South African urban culture.

Elements of deep house, jazz, soul, and lounge overlapped there.

だからAmapianoは、ある日突然生まれたジャンルではない。

Rather, it was the result of multiple musical ideas gradually coming together at township parties, DJ culture, and home studios.

Recent research has also pointed out the difficulty of reducing the origins of Amapiano to a single city or person. It has also been documented that early practitioners such as MFR Souls were involved in shaping the sound known as "ama-piano" from the early 2010s.

## Why does the brain hate repetition?

Amapianoには反復が多い。

同じコード。

同じリズム。

同じ声。

Same log drum.

But why isn't it boring?

In human musical perception, repetition is not simple copying.

When the same pattern is repeated, the brain learns the pattern.

Be able to predict what will come next.

As a result, a ""reassuring structure'' is created within the music.

But Amapiano doesn't stop at complete repetition.

There are subtle changes.

The percussion changes a little.

The accent of the log drum changes.

Voice added.

The code changes.

Sound goes out.

And back again.

This ""change through repetition'' is important.

It would be boring if it was completely predictable.

If it is not completely predictable, it will be confusing.

The state in between keeps the groove going.

Amapiano stays in the middle for a long time.

So even if the song is long, the listener can stay immersed in it.

> Amapianoは同じことを繰り返しているように見えて、実際には「予測できる部分」と「予測できない部分」を交互に動かしている。

It becomes easier to understand how music connects to the body.
