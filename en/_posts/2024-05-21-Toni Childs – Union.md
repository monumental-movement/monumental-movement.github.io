Toni Childs is a Grammy-nominated singer-songwriter.

1979年には、Terri Nunn（テリー・ナン）の前にカリフォルニア州オレンジカウンティ出身のNewWaveアクト「Berlin（ベルリン）」のリード・シンガーを短期間務めた。

Released in 1988, the album ""Union'' was Toni Childs' debut solo album and was a huge hit, going gold in the US and double platinum in Australia. In 1989, she was nominated for two Grammy Awards, Best New Artist and Best Rock Vocal Performance, and was the opening act for Bob Dylan on tour that year.
